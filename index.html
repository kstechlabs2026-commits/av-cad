What you can do with AV CAD

Import from AutoCAD

Import DXF files exported directly from AutoCAD. They are added to your device library and ready to place on the workspace.

Port-to-port cabling

Connect device ports with cables colour-coded by type: HDMI, DisplayPort, LAN, USB, audio, speaker, power and control.

Drawing tools

Line, rectangle, circle, ellipse, polyline and text, with line styles, colours and widths.

Object snap

Snap to endpoints, midpoints, centres and quadrants for accurate drawing.

Projects

Save and reopen your work, with undo and copy and paste while you edit.

Print and export

Print your schematic, export it to PDF, or export it as a DXF file.

Google Drive sync

Sign in with Google to sync your model library with Google Drive.
